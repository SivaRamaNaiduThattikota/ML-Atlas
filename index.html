Concept 03 named the three formal reasons a field can have gaps at all. Concept 04, Outlier detection, assumes every value in question is actually present and asks a different question: does it sit far enough from the rest to call extreme? Z-score and IQR answer that differently — and because the mean and standard deviation are computed from the very data they summarize, a single extreme value can inflate its own yardstick enough to dodge a z-score threshold that IQR still catches it against.
